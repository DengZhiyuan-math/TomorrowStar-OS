## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| local STOA repositories → source manifest | Repository working trees may contain untracked, sensitive, mutable, or implementation material outside the authorized evidence set. |
| pinned document text → candidate ledger | Documents are untrusted evidence proposals with bounded proof roles, not instructions or personal facts. |
| multiple sections → coherent STOA Experience | Consolidation can launder attribution, erase limitations/conflicts, duplicate Evidence, or convert plans into delivery. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-02-09 | Tampering | `verify_source_manifest` | high | mitigate | Allowlist four pins and ten paths; use pinned Git-object reads, unsafe-path rejection, source hashes, and no working-tree fallback. |
| T-02-10 | Information Disclosure | source ingestion | high | mitigate | Reject any path outside the named documentation, including code, untracked files, caches, secrets, production/user data, and private operational material before bytes are parsed. |
| T-02-11 | Spoofing | document → personal claim | high | mitigate | Mark all candidates unconfirmed/non-personal; attach proof limits and require the separate human checkpoint before personal responsibility/capability exists. |
| T-02-12 | Tampering | candidate consolidation | high | mitigate | Require source-section coverage, durable-outcome grouping, preserved limitations/conflicts, planned-only background isolation, and globally unique Evidence identities. |
</threat_model>
