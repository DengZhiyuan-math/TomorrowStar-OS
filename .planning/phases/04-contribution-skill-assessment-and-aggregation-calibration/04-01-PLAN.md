## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| persisted/model-supplied JSON to domain | Audit, policy, and assessment payloads are untrusted until strict concrete-model validation succeeds. |
| CLI to assessment authority | Local arguments may request invalid audit, admission, Skill, policy, or path transitions. |
| assessment authority to filesystem | Only validated canonical records may be atomically published inside the trusted workspace. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-01 | Tampering | assessment JSON ingress and store | high | mitigate | Revalidate through concrete frozen Pydantic models at CLI, service, write, and read boundaries; canonical-hash every authority-bearing payload. |
| T-04-02 | Elevation of privilege | proposal/admission/aggregate transitions | high | mitigate | Require completed confirmed audits, specific Skills, valid anchor bases, admitted effective assessments, and explicit policy versions; reject bypass states. |
| T-04-03 | Tampering / Information disclosure | local workspace paths | medium | mitigate | Reuse safe-ID, direct containment, symlink/hard-link rejection, atomic replacement, and exact-record validation without expanding beyond the trusted-local boundary. |
| T-04-04 | Information disclosure | CLI errors and logs | medium | mitigate | Emit stable codes, IDs, and hashes only; regression-test that raw answer sentinels never appear. |
| T-04-05 | Spoofing / Repudiation | reviewer_id and SHA-256 lineage | low | accept | This is one trusted local operator; reviewer_id is provenance and hashes are integrity identities, not authentication or signatures. |
</threat_model>
