## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| audit conclusion to Skill proposal | Confirmed prose and provenance are inputs, not permission to invent broad or adjacent capability. |
| normalization metadata to mastery | Aliases and ontology relations may identify candidates but cannot establish user capability. |
| proposed numbers to calibrated judgment | Numeric range validity is insufficient without observable anchor and exact-score bases. |
