Start with compact synthetic failing tests for a direct module API; do not invoke a command-line parser. Delete `career_sources.py` and the Phase 7 Pydantic contracts from `domain/models.py`; they have no remaining non-test consumers. Remove the `career-source` parser branch, imports, commands, flags, and error presentation from `cli.py`; retain unrelated product commands unchanged.
