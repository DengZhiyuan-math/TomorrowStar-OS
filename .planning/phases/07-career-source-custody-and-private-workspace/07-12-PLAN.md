Accept only existing text/JSON input paths or UTF-8 paste, reject other source formats, and keep any derived-text command explicit and inert. Remove commit-baseline, migration, adjudication, and verification arguments/commands. Keep the existing generic error envelope, with no public-checkout/private-workspace path or Git-detail leakage, and make no other product authority callable from this surface. Implement the scope in accordance with D-07 through D-20 as revised by the MVP addendum.
