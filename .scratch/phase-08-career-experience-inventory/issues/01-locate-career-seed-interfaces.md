# Locate the Resume and Personal-Website Seed Interfaces

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

Which local resume and personal-website artifacts are the authoritative readable seed interfaces
for proposing the initial career backbone, what structured fields and formats do they expose, and
what limits prevent treating either source as a complete or authoritative inventory?

## Comments

## Answer

Use the union of readable resume source variants and the personal website's content modules as
the initial backbone interfaces. Generated PDFs, renderers, build by-products, skills/tags, and
downstream claim-ledger judgments are not Experience seeds. Repeated entries become merge
proposals with every Source occurrence retained; neither presentation interface establishes
completeness or authority. See
[Resume and Personal-Website Seed Interfaces](../research/01-career-seed-interfaces.md).
