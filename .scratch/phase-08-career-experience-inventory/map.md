# Complete and Seal the Full-Career Experience Inventory

Label: `wayfinder:map`

## Destination

Produce one complete, source-reconciled Experience inventory revision in the private career
workspace, ready for explicit user acceptance and immutable sealing. Every known Source and
Experience Candidate has a Disposition, while unresolved items remain visible and downstream
capability claims remain out of scope.

## Notes

- Domain: Career Evidence Graph, Phase 8 — Full-Career Inventory Review and Seal.
- Canonical scope: `.planning/phases/08-full-career-inventory-review-and-seal/08-CONTEXT.md`.
- Consult `wayfinder`, `grilling`, `domain-modeling`, and `research` as each ticket requires.
- This effort explicitly carries execution inside the map: research may proceed into candidate
  generation, private coverage reporting, user reconciliation, and sealing.
- Personal content, source-derived names, paths, counts, hashes, and findings belong only in the
  private career workspace. This public local tracker records generic questions and decisions.
- Resume, personal website, repository, credential, title, and repeated wording are discovery
  clues only. They cannot establish Contribution, Skill, mastery, confidence, or responsibility.
- Formal sealing is blocked until every Source and candidate has a Disposition, the user accepts
  the exact revision, and Phase 7 has a current independent verification result.
- Refer to this map and all tickets by title, not by bare ticket number.

## Decisions so far

- [Locate the Resume and Personal-Website Seed Interfaces](issues/01-locate-career-seed-interfaces.md):
  Seed the backbone from readable resume variants and website content modules, preserving every
  occurrence as candidate lineage rather than authority.
- [Characterize the Private Source Registry for Inventory Consumption](issues/02-characterize-source-registry-shape.md):
  Consume one integrity-checked registry snapshot through the existing workspace authority and
  use it only for coverage, discovery, and lineage.
- [Derive Reviewable Repository-to-Experience Clustering Signals](issues/03-derive-repository-clustering-signals.md):
  Resolve exact repository identity separately from human-reviewed, first-party-grounded
  Experience clustering.
- [Choose the Experience Candidate and Inventory Revision Contract](issues/04-choose-experience-candidate-contract.md):
  Use one immutable source-bound proposal graph: candidate replacement edges represent edits,
  merges, and splits; complete dispositions and a hash-bound seal determine Phase 9 admission
  without placeholder Contributions or a second source authority.
- [Design the Human Review and Coverage Checkpoint](issues/05-design-the-review-and-coverage-checkpoint.md):
  Review one immutable proposal through separate candidate and Source queues, materialize an
  Inventory Revision only after all pending slots become final outcomes, derive exact ID-set
  coverage, and bind one explicit checkpoint response to the revision and coverage hashes.
